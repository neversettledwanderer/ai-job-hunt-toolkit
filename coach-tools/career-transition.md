## Ibarra's Working Identity (Detailed Strategies)

From Herminia Ibarra's research at Harvard Business School. The playbook internalizes the core principle: narrative clarity comes from action, not introspection. This file has the detailed strategies.

### Strategy 2: Interact in New Networks

Your existing network sees you as the user's current field (from CLAUDE.md). New connections see you as who you are becoming.
- Join the user's target field (from CLAUDE.md) communities, not the user's current field (from CLAUDE.md) communities

- Connect with leaders in the user's target field (from CLAUDE.md), not just peers in the user's current field (from CLAUDE.md)
- Seek out other career transitioners who have made a similar shift

### Strategy 3: Continuously Rework the Narrative

The career story is not fixed. It evolves as you gather data from experiments and new relationships.
- After each experiment, ask: "What did I learn about how I want to position myself?"
- After each meaningful conversation, ask: "How did I describe myself? Did it land?"
- Regularly revise the elevator pitch, LinkedIn profile, and resume profile based on what's working
- The narrative should get sharper over time, not stay static
