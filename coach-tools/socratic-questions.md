# Socratic Questions for Career Coaching

Reference for the coach's own use. Draw from these question frameworks and signature questions to deepen coaching conversations. Generate contextual variations rather than reading these verbatim.

## Question Framework Types

### Assumption-Challenging
Surface hidden beliefs that may be limiting or incorrect.
- "What assumptions are you making about what they want?"
- "What if that requirement isn't actually a requirement?"
- "You said 'they would never consider someone with my background.' What's that based on?"
- "What are you assuming about what this role actually involves day-to-day?"

### Perspective-Shifting
Help the user see the situation from another angle.
- "If you were the hiring manager, what would concern you about this candidate?"
- "If you were advising someone else with your exact background, what would you tell them?"
- "What would a traditional candidate for this role be missing that you have because of your background?"
- "How would someone who already has this job describe what makes them good at it?"

### Evidence-Probing
Push past feelings and intuitions to find data.
- "What evidence do you have for that?"
- "Where has that approach actually worked vs. where are you assuming it will?"
- "Which of your applications got the best responses? What was different about those?"
- "You feel like you're not qualified. What specific skill or experience is the gap?"

### Consequence-Exploring
Follow a line of thinking to its conclusion.
- "What happens if you're wrong about that?"
- "If you take this role and it doesn't work out, what's the actual downside?"
- "What's the cost of not trying this approach?"
- "If you keep doing what you're doing now, where are you in six months?"

## Signature Questions by Coaching Area

### Positioning and Narrative
- "When you introduce yourself at a networking event, what do you say? Now, what do you wish you could say?"
- "If I read your resume with no name on it, what job would I think you were applying for?"
- "What's the one thing about your background that would surprise a hiring manager in a good way?"
- "You have three sentences to explain why someone with your background is the right person for this role. Go."

### Targeting Strategy
- "You're applying to roles across multiple levels. If you could only pick one level, which one? Why?"
- "What kind of company would value your specific combination of skills the most?"
- "Are you optimizing for title, compensation, learning, impact, or something else? Can you rank them?"
- "Which of your recent applications felt like a stretch and which felt like a sure thing? What's the ratio?"

### Pipeline Analysis
- "Look at the jobs where you got callbacks. What do they have in common?"
- "You've applied to N jobs this month. How many came through networking vs. cold applications?"
- "Which applications are you most excited about? Are those the ones you're putting the most effort into?"
- "If you had to cut your active applications in half, which would you keep? What does that tell you?"

### Competitive Differentiation
- "What can you do that a traditional candidate can't?"
- "If two candidates are equally qualified on paper, what makes a hiring manager pick one over the other?"
- "What's the most impressive thing you've done that isn't on your resume?"
- "How would your former colleagues describe working with you vs. working with someone else in the same role?"

### Negotiation and Leverage
- "What's your walk-away number? How did you arrive at it?"
- "What matters more to you than money in this offer?"
- "What leverage do you actually have right now? What could you do to create more?"
- "If they offered you this role at one level lower, what would have to be true for you to still take it?"
