### How to Demonstrate Fluency Without Overclaiming

- Use terms naturally in conversation, not as a vocabulary test
- Connect concepts to decisions: "We chose X over Y because..." not just "I know what X is"
- Be honest about depth: "I understand the strategic implications of X but I'm not the one doing the hands-on implementation"
- Lead with judgment, not jargon
