# LinkedIn Outreach Templates

Master list of approved message templates for job application outreach. Customize based on the specific role, company, and your relevant experience.

---

## Category 1: Former Colleagues / People You Know (Referral Request)

**Use when:** Someone you worked with directly. Warm relationship.
**Tone:** Casual, friendly, direct about the ask.

### Template 1A: Former Direct Report or Peer (Warm)

```
Hi [Name],

Hope you're doing well at [Company]! I saw there's a [Job Title] role on the [Team/Product] team that looks like a great fit for my background, especially given my recent work [brief relevant experience].

Would you be open to referring me? I know referrals carry more weight, and I'd really appreciate it. Happy to send over my resume or chat if you'd like more context first.

Either way, would be great to catch up sometime.

[Your Name]
```

### Template 1B: Former Colleague (Haven't talked in a while)

```
Hi [Name],

Long time no see! I hope you're doing well at [Company].

I came across a [Job Title] role that I'm interested in applying to. Would you be open to referring me?

Also attached is my resume as reference. :)

Thanks!
[Your Name]
```

## Category 2: Hiring Managers

**Use when:** Reaching out to the person who would be your direct manager.
**Tone:** Professional but personable. Show you've done research.

### Template 2A: Hiring Manager (Standard)

```
Reaching out regarding your [Job Title] role

Hi [Name],

I hope you're doing well!

My name is [Your Name] and I recently applied for your [Job Title] role so I wanted to reach out to say hi and connect.

[Optional: I also wanted to point you to some relevant work: [specific link or accomplishment]]

I'm wishing you luck with the hiring process for this position and look forward to talking more.

All the best,
[Your Name]
```

## Category 3: Recruiters (Confirmed for the Role)

**Use when:** You've identified the recruiter who posted the job.
**Tone:** Professional, enthusiastic, clear value proposition.
**Action:** SEND PDF VERSION OF RESUME

### Template 3A: Recruiter (Standard)

```
Subject: Excited about your [Job Title] role

Hi [Name],

I hope this message finds you well. I'm incredibly excited about the [Job Title] opportunity at [Company]. I believe my [X]+ years of experience in [relevant area], coupled with my background in [2-3 relevant skills], align well with the responsibilities and goals for this role.

I invite you to review my attached resume to learn a bit more about me and how I can contribute to [Company]'s mission.

Looking forward to the possibility of talking more!

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

## Category 4: Recruiters (Blind Outreach)

**Use when:** You found a recruiter at the company but aren't sure if they handle this role.
**Tone:** Professional, asking for help/connection.

### Template 4A: Unknown Recruiter

```
Hi [Name],

I hope this message finds you well. My name is [Your Name], and I recently applied for the [Job Title] position at [Company]. I am curious if you could connect me with the appropriate recruiter who is handling this role?

I am excited about this opportunity and I believe my [X]+ years in [relevant area] would be a good fit, so I wanted to pass along my resume.

Thank you for your assistance.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

---

## Category 5: Network Connections

**Use when:** You have a connection who works at or knows someone at the target company.

### Template 5A: Asking for Introduction

```
Hi [Name]! I hope you're doing well!

I see you're connected to [Contact Name], [their role] at [Company]. I just applied for a [Job Title] role there. Would you feel comfortable introducing us or passing on my resume with a recommendation?

Thank you!
```

## Category 6: Employees (Informational)

**Use when:** Reaching out for intel, not necessarily a referral.

### Template 6A: Employee with Shared Background

```
Hi [Name]!

Hope you're doing well! I'm interested in the [Job Title] role at [Company] and I noticed that [shared connection] so I figured I'd reach out to say hi and see if I could pick your brain. :)

Are you happy there? How's the culture for [your role type]?

Thanks!
[Your Name]
```

## Category 7: Recruiting Lead

**Use when:** Head of talent acquisition or VP of People.

### Template 7A: Routing Request

```
Hi [Name],

I hope this message finds you well. My name is [Your Name], and I recently applied for the [Job Title] role at [Company]. I wanted to reach out directly. Would you or someone on your team be the right person to connect with about this position?

I have [X]+ years of experience in [relevant area] and believe my background would be a strong fit. I have attached my resume for reference.

Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

## Category 8: Mutual Intro

**Use when:** A target contact is 2nd degree. You message your mutual connection.

### Template 8A: Asking Mutual for Introduction

```
Hi [Mutual Connection Name],

Hope you are doing well! I wanted to ask a small favor. I noticed you are connected to [Target Contact Name] at [Company], and I am really interested in the [Job Title] role there. I just applied and I think it could be a great fit given my background in [brief context].

Would you be comfortable making a quick introduction? Even a short note would mean a lot.

No pressure at all if it is not a good time.

Thanks!
[Your Name]
```

## Category 9: Executive (C-Level or VP)

**Use when:** The contact is a C-level executive or VP. Be concise.

### Template 9A: High-Signal Pitch

```
Hi [Name],

I led [biggest relevant accomplishment with metric]. I am applying for your [Job Title] role at [Company] because [one specific sentence connecting your experience to their business].

My resume is attached if you would like context.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## Customization Notes

### For Product Management Roles, emphasize:
- Years of product leadership
- AI/ML experience
- Cross-functional team leadership
- Revenue/business impact metrics

### For Design Leadership Roles, emphasize:
- Years of design and research leadership
- Portfolio link
- Team building and scaling

### Tone & Authenticity Tips

**Avoid:**
- Generic phrases like "What excites me most about [Company] is..."
- Parroting company marketing language
- Over-the-top performative enthusiasm

**Prefer:**
- Genuine enthusiasm for the problem space
- Concrete examples from your career
- Punchy and conversational tone
