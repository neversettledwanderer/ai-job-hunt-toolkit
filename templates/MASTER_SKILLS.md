# Master Skills

All approved skills organized by category. Each skill must be 30 characters or fewer. The resume-optimizer selects 15 skills per resume variant.

## Product & Strategy
- Product Strategy & Vision
- Roadmap Prioritization
- 0-to-1 Product Development
- Product-Led Growth
- Go-to-Market Strategy

## AI & Technology
- AI/ML Product Development
- Large Language Models (LLMs)
- Agentic AI Systems
- RAG Architecture
- Model Evaluation & Testing

## Leadership & Operations

- Stakeholder Management

- Executive Communication

## Data & Analytics
- Data-Driven Decision Making
- A/B Testing & Optimization
- Product Analytics
- KPI Definition & Tracking

## Design & UX

- Design Systems & Standards
- Accessibility (WCAG)
- Prototyping & Validation

## Business & Finance
- Revenue Optimization
- Pricing Strategy
- P&L Management
- Business Case Development

---

**How to use:**
- Select 15 skills (13-15 acceptable) per resume variant
- Match skills to job requirements
- Craft new skill phrases as needed, keeping them under 30 characters
- Use consistent phrasing patterns
