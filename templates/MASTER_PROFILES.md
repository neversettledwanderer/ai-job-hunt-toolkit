# Master Profiles

Approved profile paragraphs organized by theme/angle. The resume-optimizer agent uses these as inspiration and can blend elements from multiple profiles.

## Target Length
Approximately 600-650 characters per profile paragraph.

## SaaS / General Product

Seasoned product leader with [X]+ years of experience building and scaling product organizations. Currently focused on enterprise SaaS and AI, with a track record of shipping products that drive measurable business outcomes. Combines deep technical foundations with user-centered design thinking to deliver products that are both technically sound and delightful to use. Proven ability to build cross-functional teams, define product vision, and execute from zero-to-one through scale.

## AI/ML Platform

Product leader bringing [X]+ years of product leadership to AI and machine learning. Specializes in translating complex AI capabilities into intuitive products that solve real business problems. Background in [relevant technical field] provides unique fluency with engineering teams building ML infrastructure. Track record of shipping AI-powered products, defining evaluation frameworks, and navigating the uncertainty inherent in AI product development.

## Consumer Hardware

Product leader with deep experience in hardware-software integration. Has shipped physical products used by millions of consumers, navigating the unique challenges of hardware product development: long lead times, manufacturing constraints, and the need to get it right before it ships. Combines technical depth with consumer empathy to deliver products that work reliably in the real world.
