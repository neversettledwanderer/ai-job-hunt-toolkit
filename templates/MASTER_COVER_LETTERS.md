# Master Cover Letters

Archive of the best cover letters written during the job search. These serve as tone and structure references for the cover-letter-optimizer agent. Maximum 5 letters in the archive at any time.

**These are examples of voice, structure, and quality -- not templates to copy.**

## [Company Name] -- [Role Title]
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Verdict:** SENT AS-IS
**Why it's here:** [Brief note on what makes this letter effective]

Dear [Company] Team,

[Opening paragraph expressing genuine connection to the company's mission. Not parroting their marketing. A real belief stated in your own voice.]

[Evidence paragraph 1. A specific story from your career that demonstrates relevant capability. Bold header followed by the story. Setup, what you did, what happened. Tight and specific.]

[Evidence paragraph 2. A different story proving a different skill. Each evidence paragraph should do a different job.]

[Closing paragraph. Forward-looking, confident but not arrogant. What excites you about contributing.]

Sincerely,
[YOUR_NAME]

---

**Archive rules:**
- Maximum 5 letters
- When adding a 6th, nominate one to retire
- Letters are kept for voice reference, not content reuse
- Each letter should demonstrate a different positioning angle
